Seat Saver — account and data deletion request

You can delete your Seat Saver host account and all of its data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

1. From inside the app (immediate)

If you still have the app installed:

  • Open Seat Saver and sign in as a host
  • Go to Host dashboard → Account
  • Choose Delete account and type DELETE to confirm

Deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone.

2. By email (if you have uninstalled the app)

Email kushaldayanand243@gmail.com from the email address your account is registered to, with the subject “Account deletion request”.

We use the address you send from to confirm the request is yours, so please send it from the registered account. Your account will be deleted within 30 days, and in practice usually within a few working days. You will get a confirmation once it is done.

What gets deleted

Deleting a host account permanently removes:

  • your host profile — business name, contact email and sign-in
  • every event you have created, past and upcoming
  • all floor layouts: sections, tables and seats
  • all menus and dining rounds
  • all guest bookings for your events, including seat history, party sizes, check-ins and the phone numbers those guests provided
  • all feedback responses and guest call requests for your events
  • any pending or sent reminder records for your events

This is not a deactivation. The records are deleted from the database, not hidden or flagged. Nothing above can be restored afterwards, by you or by us.

What is kept, and why

A guest account is identified by that guest’s own phone number and is shared across every caterer they have ever booked with. If a guest who attended one of your events has also booked with a different caterer, their guest record itself stays — deleting it would destroy that other caterer’s booking history, which is not yours to delete. All of your events’ booking data for that guest is still deleted.

Guests who only ever booked with you are removed completely, phone number included.

Deleting a guest account

If you are a guest rather than a caterer and want your phone number and booking history removed, email kushaldayanand243@gmail.com from the phone number’s registered account, or include the phone number you booked with so it can be matched.
