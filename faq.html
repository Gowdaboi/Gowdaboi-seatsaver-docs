Seat Saver — Frequently asked questions

For caterers and venues evaluating the app. Last updated: 17 September 2026

Your data

Where is the data stored?

In a Postgres database hosted by Supabase on Amazon Web Services in the Mumbai region (ap-south-1). The database, sign-in service and account records all live in that region, so guest and event data is held in India.

Supabase encrypts all stored data with AES-256 and all traffic in transit with TLS. Supabase itself holds SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001 certification.

Two things leave the database to do their job: guest verification codes and round reminders are sent through Twilio, which receives the guest’s phone number and the message text (guest name, event name, round number and a cancel link).

How much data is stored?

Very little, and none of it is sensitive beyond a phone number.

  • About the caterer: business name and contact email. Passwords are handled by the sign-in service and are never visible to us.
  • About each guest: phone number and, if they give one, a name.
  • About each event: the floor layout (sections, tables, seats), the menu, the dining rounds, and each booking — party size, round, seat and whether the guest arrived.

No photos, location, contacts, payment details or identity documents are collected. The camera is used only to read a QR code at check-in, and the image is decoded on the device and never recorded or uploaded.

In storage terms a booking is a few hundred bytes. A 1,000-guest wedding is well under a megabyte.

What do you do with this data?

We use it to run your events, and that’s all:

  • seating guests and managing dining rounds;
  • releasing no-show seats and offering them to waiting guests;
  • sending guests their verification code and a reminder before their round;
  • emailing the caterer about their own account, such as confirmation and password reset.

The app shows no adverts and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code. Guest phone numbers are used only for that guest’s own verification and reminders — never for marketing.

The full terms are in the privacy policy.

Who can see my events and guest list?

Only you. Each caterer’s events are walled off from every other caterer’s inside the database, not just hidden by the app’s screens. So a bug in the app cannot show one caterer another caterer’s event.

A guest can see the floor plan and menu of the event they scanned into, plus their own booking. They cannot see other guests’ names or phone numbers.

How long is data kept, and can it be deleted?

Event records are kept for as long as your account exists, so your past events — floor, menu, bookings and recap — stay available to reuse and look back on. Old events can be archived to hide them without losing anything.

Deleting your account is immediate and permanent. It removes the account, every event, and all guest bookings and phone numbers tied to those events. Do it in the app under Host dashboard → Account → Delete account, or follow Delete your account if the app is no longer installed.

Guests can ask for their own number and booking history to be removed by emailing anyproblemapps@gmail.com.

Scale

How many guests and users can the app handle?

The app puts no limit on the number of guests, events, tables or rounds, so a large wedding works the same way as a small party.

It is also light on resources. Guests looking at the floor plan or menu load the page and then disconnect, rather than staying connected. And a booking takes up only a few hundred bytes.

The AnyProblem Apps team runs the hosting and scales it to your event volume, so there is nothing for you to set up or manage.

Can I run more than one event, or have several staff logged in?

A caterer account can hold any number of events, and past events stay available for reference.

Today there is one login per caterer business. Separate staff accounts with their own permissions are not built yet.

Guests on the day

Do guests need to download an app or create an account?

No. Guests scan a QR code at the venue and the app opens in their phone’s browser. They confirm their phone number with a one-time code — no password, no email, no signup.

Walk-ins without a smartphone can still be seated: the caterer assigns them a seat directly.

What devices does it run on?
  • Guests: a modern phone browser.
  • Caterers: the Android app, currently in testing on Google Play before public release, or any browser on a phone, tablet or laptop.

There is no native iPhone app yet.

What happens if the venue has poor internet?

Seat Saver needs a connection — mobile data is enough. Seating is shared live between the caterer and every guest, so there is no offline mode.

When a connection drops, the caterer’s rounds screen shows how long ago it last updated, so staff can see the data is stale rather than trusting it. It refreshes by itself once the connection returns.

What happens when a booked guest doesn’t turn up?

Once a round starts, a seat not checked in within the event’s no-show time (5 minutes unless the caterer changes it) is released. It is then offered to waiting guests one at a time, in order. Each guest gets a short window to accept (1 minute by default) before it passes to the next person.

Messaging and pricing

Do guests get SMS reminders?

Yes. Guests get their sign-in code by SMS. Before their round they also get a reminder with a one-tap link to cancel and free the seat. The reminder goes out 5 minutes ahead by default, and you can change that.

What do I pay for?

Only the Seat Saver app. Hosting, the database and SMS messaging are handled by the AnyProblem Apps team. You don’t need your own accounts with any other service, and there are no separate bills.
